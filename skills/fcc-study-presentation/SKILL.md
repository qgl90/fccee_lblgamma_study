# Study presentation

Read `docs/agents/PRESENTATION_WORKFLOW.md`, the stage's `howto/` guide, and
its PI review note. Use `presentations/stage1_v3_review/` as the local Beamer
example. Build a new snapshot-named deck from plots already traced to frozen
inputs; record source paths and hashes in its README. Keep the original plot
legends and units visible, and distinguish candidate/event counts, normalized
shapes, and expected yields. Include the question, method, exact comparison,
observed effect, statistical limit, and decision for the PI. Build and inspect
the PDF before presenting it.
