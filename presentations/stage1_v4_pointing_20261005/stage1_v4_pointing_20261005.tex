\begin{frame}{Procedure, steps 1--3: build the nominal hit}
\small
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Read Stage 1 candidate fields.} Take the reconstructed photon energy and momentum, its matched truth photon momentum and production point, the reconstructed PV, and the effective ECAL surface parameters. The photon energy response is already applied in Stage 1.
\item \textbf{Choose the nominal hit anchor.} Project the reconstructed photon momentum, whose direction assumes production at the origin, from $(0,0,0)$ to the first forward crossing of the effective cylinder or endcap. This saved \texttt{reco\_hit} is the hit position used by the nominal offline study.
\item \textbf{Keep a truth-ray comparison.} Independently project the matched truth momentum from the truth production point. This \texttt{truth\_hit} is useful for geometry and closure checks; it does not replace the nominal reco-derived hit.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{frame}{Procedure, steps 4--6: emulate pointing and measure displacement}
\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
\column{0.57\textwidth}
\footnotesize
\begin{enumerate}
\setcounter{enumi}{3}
\item \textbf{Attach the truth direction to the reco hit.} Normalize the matched truth momentum to obtain $\hat n_{truth}$. The truth production point sets neither the nominal hit nor a new photon origin.
\item \textbf{Make a resolution hypothesis.} Draw two Gaussian offsets in orthogonal directions perpendicular to $\hat n_{truth}$; combine them with that direction and renormalize. Repeat for per-component $\sigma=0.5,0.7,0.9,1.1,1.3,1.5$ mrad. Reuse deterministic draws across hypotheses and candidates sharing a photon.
\item \textbf{Calculate the displacement.} Set $\vec p_{hyp}=E_{reco}\hat n_{smear}$. Use the fixed reco hit and reconstructed PV to compute $IP_{3D}=|(\vec x_{reco\ hit}-\vec x_{PV})\times\hat n_{smear}|$ and transverse $|d_0|$. Apply and scan cuts offline after BDT and the chosen other selections.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{frame}{Saved inputs and pilot geometry check}
\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.54\textwidth}
For each reconstructed $\Lambda_b$ candidate the v4 tuple keeps:
\begin{itemize}
\item measured photon $E_{\rm reco},(p_x,p_y,p_z)_{\rm reco}$ and reco index;
\item matched truth photon $(p_x,p_y,p_z,E)$ and production vertex;
\item predicted ECAL hit from the displaced truth ray and, separately, from the current origin-based reco direction;
\item reconstructed PV and effective surface parameters.
\end{itemize}
Smear the truth direction in two orthogonal tangent directions with Gaussian component width $\sigma=0.5$–$1.5$ mrad (six points), then set $\vec p_{\gamma}^{\,hyp}=E_{\rm reco}\hat n_{\rm smear}$. Reuse the same random draw for a photon shared by candidates. Evaluate $IP_{3D}=|(\vec x_{hit}-\vec x_{PV})\times\hat n|$ and $|d_0|$ offline after BDT.
\end{column}
